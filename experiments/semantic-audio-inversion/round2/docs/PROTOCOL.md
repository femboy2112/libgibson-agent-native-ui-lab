# Round 2 execution protocol

Round-1 source, metrics, thresholds, dependencies, receipts and sealed holdout remain
unchanged. The dependency is LibGibson c2f6483d92fe2b351e6cd50936a97d8cdf73cb79.
All real music, event transcriptions, stems and generated covers remain outside git.

The experiment distinguishes three questions: integrity of the supplied measurements;
admission by the public constrained lift; and survival of identity when measured again
from PCM. None entails the next. A rejected candidate stays rejected.

Before real-song execution, compare six fresh generated development items (three worlds,
two seeds, 32 beats) through full mix and isolated roles. Reuse the frozen Round-1 DSP
instrument for the matched isolation contrast. A separate monophonic YIN route tests
whether transcription is also a bottleneck. Both are development probes, not a holdout.
Record note precision/recall/F1, line relations, temporal harmonic agreement, and groove.
Greater stem F1 supports isolation only on these items; floor relations limit the claim.
No duration/onset/pitch truth is passed to an audio observer. Evaluation may use a
declared time transport; timing-conditioned results must be identified as such.

Real candidates use the same compiler policy for every source, all viable worlds, and
two fixed seeds. No song-specific repair or threshold. Compilation retains ambiguity
and lowered ceilings. Public API refusal is evidence, not permission to remove a pin.
Compare per-axis residuals; order structural failures before production distances.
If all candidates fail observational structural gates, `best` means a diagnostic
listening candidate, never a successful cover. Production fitting is authorized only
after observational structural conformance, with a preserved raw render and a fresh
post-production observation. Failed gates cannot be bought with timbral similarity.

The old 30-item holdout is never read. A Round-2 holdout is generated only after an
explicit implementation/configuration freeze, then sealed without analysis; development
changes before freeze do not consume a holdout.

Rivals: (1) isolation resolves the principal wall; (2) pitch/onset inference remains
limiting; (3) source-map errors manufacture agreement; (4) the public lift cannot express
the observed object. Source/Demucs/librosa routes share provenance and cannot count as
independent corroboration. No phase model or quantum interpretation is used.
