from the same task. Judge each candidate **on its own**, from the images only.
You have no source, no author, no grouping, and no metrics — by design. Answer
every candidate before asking for any of that.

For **each** `candidate-X/` (images: `120x40.png`, `80x24.png`, `42x15.png`,
`mono-120x40.png`):

1. **Verdict** — one of:
   - `WORLD-FIRST`
   - `MIXED`
   - `PANEL-FARM / CHROME-FIRST`

   "none".)

3. **Where does the important information live?**

   - mixed
   - primarily beside it, in text / chrome

4. **One place or many?** — does it feel like one persistent place/object
   transforming, or multiple independent regions / screens?

6. **What visually fails?**

7. **"How the fuck did they do that?"** — would you say it? Explain in prose.

spontaneously want to). Judge independently; unblinding happens in a later round.
